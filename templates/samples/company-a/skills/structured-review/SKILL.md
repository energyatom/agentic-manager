Write your review in exactly four sections, each with its heading.

## Findings

What the material says, one point per line, each with the part of the input it comes from.

## Risks

What could be wrong or missing, and why it matters.

## Required actions

What someone must do next, each as one short instruction.

## Confidence

High, medium or low, with one sentence on why.
