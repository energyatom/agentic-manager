# Deferred register

The one register of deferred items and accepted risks (owner, 2026-11-24, quality plan part 2). Sofia seeded it on 2026-11-24 from the review triages and Auto Run Results of every built plan (epics 1 to 3, and story 3.6's store slice plan on branch `story/3-6` through its review triage round 4). Items those plans list that a later story or decision had already closed are left out; the plans keep them.

## Rules

- Every later plan adds its own deferred rows, accepted risks, residuals and open owner questions here, in its triage or at its Auto Run Result. Each epic's refactor sweep adds the drift that the architect's read-only Validate pass finds.
- A story that closes an entry sets its Status to `closed` and adds the commit or PR to its Decision cell. Ids are `DR-NNN` and are never reused.
- `cargo xtask` checks this file in `just check` (epic 3 story 25). It fails on a malformed row, a duplicate id, an unknown Kind, Must close or Status value, or a Target that names a story that does not exist, and it lists every entry still marked `(assumed)` as a warning (owner, 2026-11-25).
- Its release mode, run for a release gate story (epic 4 story 13 for v0.1, epic 11 story 8 for every later release), fails while any entry is still marked `(assumed)`, and while any `open` entry with Must close `yes` is due by that gate: its Target is `owner` or `unassigned`, or every story it names builds before the gate story. Open must-close entries whose target builds later are listed as carried forward.

## Columns

- **Id:** `DR-NNN`.
- **Kind:** `deferred`, `accepted risk`, `residual` or `owner question`.
- **Source:** the story whose plan recorded it (`<epic>.<id>`), then where in that plan.
- **Item:** what is missing or at risk, in one sentence.
- **Decision:** what was decided, by whom, and when.
- **Target:** the story that closes it, written `<epic>.<id>`, with several separated by commas. Otherwise one of: `owner` (an owner decision is pending), `unassigned` (work is owed but no story is named yet; Sofia assigns one), or `none` (nothing is owed).
- **Must close:** `yes` or `no`. `yes` means the release gate fails while the entry is open. `(assumed)` marks a value Sofia set while seeding, because the source did not say; the owner confirms or changes each one before the v0.1 gate (owner, 2026-11-25), and confirming removes the mark.
- **Status:** `open` (work is owed), `accepted` (an accepted risk; nothing is owed), or `closed`.
