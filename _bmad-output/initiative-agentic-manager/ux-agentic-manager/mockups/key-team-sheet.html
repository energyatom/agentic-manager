Team side sheet

Opened by Return on a selected team box. Same sheet shell as the Seat sheet. Hand-offs are what moves between departments in workflows; grants are what a team shares with other teams and what it receives. Grants are always read-only and appear as dotted accent lines, hand-offs as dashed arrows, both hidden at rest: selecting a department draws its hand-offs, and grant lines are drawn only on hover and behind this sheet, the home of grants.
