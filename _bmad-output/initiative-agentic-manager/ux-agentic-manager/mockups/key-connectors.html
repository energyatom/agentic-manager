Connectors and Sources

Connectors are generic building blocks (Web Search, HTTP Reader, Crawler, API, Feed, Local Repository, Knowledge, Database, MCP, Actions). A Source is one configured use of a connector, owned by a seat: what it reads, where it loads, when, and within which limits. Sources run without a model; everything they load is outside content.

3 · Add Source, Scope step, with Test connector. Six steps: Connector · Account · Scope · Output (table, documents or knowledge; field mapping and key) · Schedule & limits · Test. A Crawler's scope is start pages, paths to follow and never follow, depth, a hard page budget, pace, the rendering switch (off here: plain pages) and the site-terms check. Test connector checks reachability, robots.txt, allow-list and size, and shows a sample page as untrusted content with its field mapping; it writes nothing and states its cost. New hosts make this a widening change: Finish becomes "Review and add…" and the two-step confirmation.
